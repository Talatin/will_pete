#System

# Must Haves

- **Title Screen**
	- Cute wallpaper with a flashing "Start Game"

- Level Selection
	- Overworld
		- Different Levels selectable
		- Current level autoselected at start

- **Settings**
	- Audio
		- Different volume sliders for Master, Sfx, Music
	- Input
		- Rebind your inputs to better fit your playstyle
	+ Video
		+ Brightness
	- Handicaps
		- Color blind options 
		- Read text out loud








# Player login 
I'm a fan of character controlled menus.
So the 2 players would login with their respective controllers and each gain control of a character (will or pete) with their rifle.
They can then shoot at the different buttons in the menu to trigger corresponding events.

They can also play around with the controls and get to know the game in a playful way

# Default Menu
Start Screen -> New Game, Load Game, Settings, Level Selection
Load Game -> Level Selection Screen with newest level hovered
Settings -> Opens a new screen with the games settings (Audio, video, handicap, etc)
